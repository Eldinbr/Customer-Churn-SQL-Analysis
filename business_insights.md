# Business Insights

Run the SQL scripts against the dataset and record the final results here.

## Key Questions
- Which contract type has the highest churn?
- Are short-tenure customers more likely to leave?
- Does monthly pricing relate to churn?
- Does technical support relate to retention?
- Which payment methods have higher churn?
- How much monthly revenue is at risk?
- Which high-value customers should receive retention activity?

## Recommended Retention Actions
1. Prioritise month-to-month customers.
2. Strengthen onboarding during the first 12 months.
3. Investigate high monthly charges among churned customers.
4. Promote technical support.
5. Target high-value, high-risk customers.
6. Monitor churn by contract, service and payment method.
